## Why Play It

A busy ztn arena with heavy weapons, multiple rails, a BFG, and a Quad for servers that want fireworks.

## Where It Came From

The Killing Machine is one of ztn's early Quake II deathmatch maps and part of the Netpack-era canon. It is less austere than the tight duel maps and better suited to noisy rotation play.

## How It Plays

Ideal for casual FFA and 2v2. The BFG and Quad are not shy, so hosts wanting strict duel purity may prefer Painkiller or The Rage.

## Why Play It

A compact pressure cooker built around fast vertical returns, clean rocket fights, and constant Mega Health tension.

## Where It Came From

Preacher's Quake II Aerowalk adaptation carried one of deathmatch's most famous duel layouts into the Strogg era. The Muff Mode version keeps the immediate read of the original while rebuilding it for the rerelease toolchain.

## How It Plays

Best when the lobby wants a map that rewards timing, denial, and quick escapes. With six starts it can support a small FFA, but it shines brightest when two players are fighting over the central routes.
